# Position scope

Refines [Position tracking](position-tracking.md):

- **One position per book.** Each book has Continue. The library's top button, and a headset or lock-screen play after a kill or reboot, resume the last-played book.
- **No milliseconds.** A position is a book, chapter and sentence. Resume always starts at the start of a sentence, or one sentence earlier after more than 5 minutes away, so the offset was never used.
- **Position history** keeps the last 20 positions per book that you jumped away from. It records on tap to jump and on picking a chapter, not on every sentence change.

# Smaller MVP choices

Stated during Phase 2 planning on 2026-11-09, and accepted:

- Wuxiaworld stays out of the MVP. Its plugin needs protobufjs in the bundle.
- Chapter text is kept for good once fetched. It's small, and positions depend on its sentences.
- Changing the voice makes new sentence audio from the current sentence on. Audio in the old voice is deleted.
- Playback speed is one setting for all books, applied live by the player without making new audio.

# Spike 2: playback

Status, 2026-11-09: designed, and the test app in `spikes/playback/` was being written when work paused. That code is unreviewed and uncommitted. Check that it builds with `./run.sh build` and matches this design before using it.

## What it proves

[Decision 4](../decisions/position-tracking.md) on your A26: each sentence is one Media3 playlist item, generated by Phonon just ahead of playback. Playback waits instead of ending when it reaches an unready sentence, your place survives every interruption, and the next chapter is fetched with the screen off.

## Design

- **App:** `app.lantern.spike.playback`, set up like `spikes/engine/` (AGP 9.5.1, compileSdk 37, minSdk 28, arm64 only, `libptts_ffi.so` from `.bench/`), plus Media3 1.11.1 `exoplayer` and `session`. Plain views, no Compose.
- **Service:** a `MediaSessionService` owns the ExoPlayer, with speech audio attributes, audio focus handled, pausing when headphones disconnect (`setHandleAudioBecomingNoisy`), and `WAKE_MODE_NETWORK`. Spike 1 showed a background app gets almost no CPU, so generation runs inside this service.
- **Sentences:** item URIs are `lantern://sentence/<chapter>/<index>`. A custom `DataSource` maps each to `cacheDir/audio/<chapter>/<index>.wav` and waits until the file exists, so the player sits in BUFFERING instead of ending. The generator writes `.wav.tmp` and renames when done.
- **Generator:** one thread, Phonon at 2 threads with the voice Everly. It makes the first unready sentence at or after the playing one, then the next chapter, never further ahead. Only the current and next chapters' audio is kept.
- **Chapters:** Ode of the Brave from AllNovelFull (`novgo.net`), fetched with plain HTTP and the same cleanup as the Spike 1 chapters. This is throwaway; Spike 3 replaces it with plugins. When a chapter starts, the next one is fetched, with up to 5 retries a minute apart, and appended to the playlist.
- **Position:** chapter and sentence, saved with `SharedPreferences.commit()` on every sentence change, pause, task removal and service destroy. Resume starts at the saved sentence, or one earlier after more than 5 minutes away. `onPlaybackResumption` plus Media3's `MediaButtonReceiver` cover headset and lock-screen play after a kill or reboot.
- **Log:** every event goes to `events.log` in the app's files folder, with the screen state. `run.sh log` and `run.sh pull` fetch it.
- **Left out:** the one-item lock-screen view (`ForwardingSimpleBasePlayer`), speed control and highlighting.

## Test plan

1. Plugged in: `./run.sh install` and `./run.sh push-model`.
2. Unplugged, about an hour with the screen off from chapter 1: no ENDED state, no long waits, generation speed close to Spike 1's RTF 0.33, next chapters fetched with the screen off, and you listen for clicks or gaps between sentences.
3. Interruptions, each followed by resuming within one sentence: pause, app switch, a phone call (someone needs to call you), turning off Bluetooth headphones, `adb` kill, force stop and reboot.
4. Generation keeps its speed in the service while you use other apps that don't play audio.

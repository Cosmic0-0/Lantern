Chapter 42. The Sealed Gate. Mira counted the steps under her breath. There were 1,024 of them, and she had climbed 317 before the lantern began to fail. "Stop," said Kael. "What?" "Listen." Far below, something heavy dragged across the stone. Mira held her breath. The sound stopped, then started again, closer this time. She had been told the old city had stood empty for 300 years, but the torches along the wall were still warm. "No," she whispered. "That isn't possible." Kael drew his sword and pointed at the narrow door ahead. It was marked with a faded crest, and beneath it someone had scratched a date: March 3rd, 1847. "We have ten minutes, maybe less," he said. "If that thing reaches us before the gate opens, run. Don't wait for me." She wanted to argue. Instead, she pressed her palm against the cold iron and began to speak the words her mother had taught her, quietly at first, and then louder as the floor began to shake.
